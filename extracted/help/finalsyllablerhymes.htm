Final Syllable Rhymes

A final syllable rhyme is a word whose final syllable rhymes with the the final syllable of word you enter in the Word to Find box, regardless of the lexical stress patterns in the words. For example, a final syllable rhyme search on "porcupine" will result in words like "tine", "align", "frankenstein", etc.  In other words, the results will include words whose final syllable rhymes with the final syllable of the word you entered.

 

Note: A "perfect rhyme" and a "final syllable rhyme" are the same if the lexical emphasis is on the final syllable of the word. For example, the word "revolt" has the emphasis on the final syllable "volt".  Therefore, only this syllable is used in a perefect rhyme. However, in the word "myopic" has its lexical emphasis on the second-to-last syllable, "op". Therefore, both of the last two syllables are used in the perfect rhyme.
