Reverse Rhymes

A "reverse rhyme" may also be called a "reverse perfect rhyme." It is a word that, in reverse, rhymes with the reverse of the search term. For example, a search using the word "capricious", which has its lexical stress on the first syllable, will result in words such as "comely" and "configure." A reverse rhyme search for the word "Revert", which has its lexical stress on the second syllable, will result in words like "reverse," "reversal," etc.

 

Note: A "reverse first syllable rhyme" and a "reverse rhyme" are the same if the lexical emphasis is on the first syllable of the word. For example, the word "capricious" has the emphasis on the first syllable "cap", therefore, only this syllable is used in a reverse rhyme. However, in the word "revert" has its lexical emphasis on the second syllable. Therefore, both syllables are used in the reverse rhyme.
