Reverse First Syllable Rhymes

A reverse first syllable rhyme is a word whose first syllable rhymes in reverse with the first syllable of the word you enter in the Word to Find box, regardless of the lexical stress patterns of the words. For example, the first syllable of the word "capricious" sounds like "kuh." Doing a reverse first syllable rhyme search for the word "capricious" results in words whose first syllable, in reverse, sounds like "uck" (e.g., "comely" and "configure," etc.).

 

Reverse first syllable rhymes are especially useful for finding words that begin with the same sounds.

 

Note: A "reverse first syllable rhyme" and a "reverse rhyme" are the same if the lexical emphasis is on the first syllable of the word. For example, the word "capricious" has the emphasis on the first syllable "cap", therefore, only this syllable is used in a reverse rhyme. However, in the word "revert" has its lexical emphasis on the second syllable. Therefore, both syllables are used in the reverse rhyme.
