A Comprehensive Reference Tool

Rhymesaurus™ is an invaluable reference tool for writers.   Rhymesaurus is especially useful for poets, songwriters, advertising copy writers, and anyone else who often needs to find "just the right word."

 

Rhymesaurus combines the following tools into a single easy-to-use interface:

 

·a 115,000 word dictionary with full definitions
·a thesaurus containing 1000 word categories
·a 120,000 word rhyming dictionary with 21 different rhyme types
·a "Sounds Like" reference for instant lookup of  words that sound alike
·WordSurf: a powerful tool for surfing relationships between words
·a pattern matching tool for finding words based on syllable count and lexical stress and beginning and ending letters

 

Rhymesaurus' rhyming dictionary allows you to do non-standard rhymes, such as final syllable rhymes, reverse rhymes, homophones, sight rhymes and more.  Additionally, Rhymesaurus' hypertext interface allows you to quickly find the definitions, synonyms and rhymes of the words in your search results.  Rhymesaurus also provides browser-like "history" buttons, allowing you to go back and view the results of previous searches.
