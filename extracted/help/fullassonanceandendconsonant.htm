Assonance and End

The Assonance and End feature of the rhyming dictionary shows you all words the contain the same vowel sounds, and end with the same consonant sound, regardless of lexical stress and number of syllables.  For example, searching on the word "vatican" will give you a result that includes "african," "ambition," "transcription," and more.

 

Note: This feature matches the vowel sounds, and the final consonant sound not the exact letters.
