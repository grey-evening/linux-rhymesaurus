The Rhymesaurus Dictionary is a fast, easy tool to use. Select "Dictionary" in the Search/Link Action area and type the word you wish to look up in the Word to Find box. Then, click on the Search button and definitions of the word will appear in the search result box. "Part of speech" notation (whether the word is used as a noun or verb, etc.) precedes each definition.
