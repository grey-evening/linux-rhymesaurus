About Rhymesaurus' References

This release of Rhymesaurus uses public domain reference works in its database.  These include an electronic thesaurus derived from the version of Roget's Thesaurus published in 1911, an electronic dictionary derived from the version of 1913 edition of Webster's dictionary produced by Project Gutenberg, and the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary, produced by Carnegie Mellon University, and WordNet, produced by Princeton University.

 

Because the dictionary and thesaurus are both quite old editions, they do not contain many modern words.  Nevertheless, they still contain enough material to be very useful references.  The CMU Pronouncing Dictionary contains pronunciations for many words that are not in the dictionary or thesaurus, such as proper names, foreign words, etc.  All of the references used in this program likely contain errors.  Purple Room Publishing makes no warrantees regarding the correctness, completeness, or suitability of the content of these reference works.
