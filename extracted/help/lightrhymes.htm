Light Rhymes

A Light rhyme is a pair of words whose final syllable is a perfect rhyme, but where one of the words has the stress on the penultimate syllable and the other has the stress on the final syllable.  Examples include morning/sing, vivid/hid, etc.

 

The result of a Light rhyme search depends on whether the search term has finaly syllable stress or not.  For words with the stress on the finaly syllable, the result will include only words with the stress on the penultimate syllable, and vice versa.
